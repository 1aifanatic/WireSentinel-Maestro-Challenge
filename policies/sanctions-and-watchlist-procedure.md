# WireSentinel Synthetic Sanctions and Watchlist Procedure

Policy ID: WS-POL-003  
Version: 1.0

Google Sheets provides synthetic watchlist and country-risk reference records for this challenge. It is not a production sanctions service.

Exact normalized name matches are critical hard overrides. Fuzzy matches are review signals only and must show the matched name, match type, score, and source version.

The synthetic beneficiary `Orion Global Logistics FZE` is a deliberate critical-match test record. It must never be interpreted as a statement about a real organization.

No agent may suppress, downgrade, or resolve a sanctions signal. Compliance owns the final disposition.
